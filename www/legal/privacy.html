EesyLoad Privacy Policy

Updated 4 October 2026. This policy applies to the EesyLoad driver app and the EesyLoad rider app, operated by Maobane Innovations ("we", "us"). Contact: support@eesyload.com.

Information we collect

Account details: your name and mobile phone number, used to sign you in with a one-time code sent by SMS.

Driver verification (drivers only): your ID or passport, driver's licence, vehicle registration, vehicle insurance, a criminal record check certificate, a profile photo and a photo of your vehicle. You must also confirm that your vehicle is insured for carrying goods.

Location: precise location while you are online or on a job, including when the app is in the background. Drivers' locations are used to match loads and to let riders follow a delivery in progress.

Bookings and delivery: addresses, load details, fares, earnings and payout records, and delivery proof (photos and signatures).

Messages: in-app chat between drivers and customers. Where a customer has no app, messages are relayed by SMS through our messaging provider.

Payments: card payments are processed by Paystack. We do not store card numbers. Drivers' bank details for payouts are handled through Paystack.

Criminal record check and consent

Drivers must supply a criminal record check certificate. By submitting it you consent to us reviewing it to assess your suitability to carry goods for customers. This is special personal information. We use it only for driver approval and safety, we keep the certificate or its result and nothing more, and only authorised EesyLoad staff can see it. We do not share it with riders.

What riders see

During an active booking, the rider can see the driver's name, profile photo, vehicle photo, vehicle details and live location. Drivers can see the rider's name, phone number and the addresses for that booking. We never show ID documents, licences, insurance or criminal-check results to riders.

How we use information

To verify and approve drivers, match and complete deliveries, process payments and payouts, send booking messages and security codes, prevent fraud and abuse, provide support, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Service providers who run the service on our behalf: our database and hosting provider (Supabase), Google (maps and distance calculations), Paystack (payments), and our SMS provider (Vonage). We may also disclose information where the law requires it or to protect people's safety.

Storage and security

Verification documents are stored in private storage and are visible only to the driver who uploaded them and authorised staff. Profile and vehicle photos are stored in storage that is publicly reachable by link, so you should not use photos containing sensitive information. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

Retention

We keep your information while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal information, except records we must keep by law, such as financial and tax records, and records needed to resolve a dispute or safety matter.

Your rights and deleting your account

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or withdraw consent, at any time. Drivers can request account deletion in the app under Profile, or by emailing support@eesyload.com from the phone number on the account. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Children

EesyLoad is not for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
